THE OFFICIAL (ISH) LEGISLATION

The Boardfest Code of Conduct

Four articles. Zero legal authority. Surprisingly important.

🤫

ARTICLE I — THE MIDNIGHT ACCORD

At midnight, all gaming operations must relocate to the kitchen and the volume must come down. Being drunk is not an exemption from the laws of acoustics. If whisky-fuelled social deduction cannot survive the decibel limit, choose a quieter game. 😂

🍺

ARTICLE II — THE FRIDGE CONVENTION

Any beverage removed from the fridge must be replaced from the stock. Keep the cold drinks cold. We live in a society.

🧹

ARTICLE III — THE CLEANLINESS PACT

Keep the place clean and tidy. Leave no trace — except the emotional damage of a perfectly timed betrayal.

✅

ARTICLE IV — THE LABOUR AGREEMENT

Everyone signs up for a cooking or washing-up job. Many hands make light work. All hands also appreciate clean plates.
